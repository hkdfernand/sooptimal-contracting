Process

How the job goes

You get a written scope before we start, and the same person on site every day.

  1. Scope the finish levelWe look at the room, the lighting and the paint you plan to use, and recommend Level 4 or Level 5.
  2. Tape and bedPaper tape on flats and inside corners, bead on outside corners.
  3. Fill and finish coatsEach coat wider than the one before, with full drying time between.
  4. Sand and check with lightWe sand, then check every wall with a raking light and fix what shows before primer.

Questions

Taping and finishing FAQ

What is the difference between a Level 4 and Level 5 finish?

Level 4 is taped joints with three coats of compound, sanded smooth. It suits flat and matte paint. Level 5 adds a thin skim coat over the entire surface, which hides joints under glossy paint or strong window light.

Do you hang drywall as well?

We hang board for repairs, small rooms and closing up renovation openings. For large new-build boarding we finish after your boarder.

How long does taping a basement take?

Most basements take four to six working days for tape, three coats and sanding, mostly because each coat needs to dry.

Can you fix another contractor's taping?

Yes. Visible seams, ridges and bubbled tape can usually be corrected with re-taping where needed and a skim coat.

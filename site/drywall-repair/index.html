Process

How the job goes

You get a written scope before we start, and the same person on site every day.

  1. Photos or a visitText us photos of the damage. Most repairs can be quoted the same day from pictures and rough measurements.
  2. Cut out and rebuildLoose and wet material comes out. New board goes in on solid backing, not a mesh patch over a hole.
  3. Three coats, feathered wideTape coat, fill coat and finish coat, each one wider than the last, so there is no ridge to catch the light.
  4. Prime and paintPatches are spot-primed so they do not flash through the topcoat. We can paint the wall or the whole room.

Questions

Drywall repair FAQ

Can you quote a drywall repair from photos?

Yes. Send two or three photos and the approximate size of the damage by text to (343) 305-1787. Most small and medium repairs get a firm price from photos. Water damage and ceilings sometimes need a visit first.

How long does a drywall repair take?

A typical patch needs three coats of compound with drying time between them. Small repairs are usually done in one to two visits using fast-setting compound. Larger sections take two to three days including primer and paint.

Will I be able to see the patch afterward?

A patch shows when it is not feathered wide enough or not primed. We feather each coat well past the repair and prime before paint. On walls with sheen or strong side light we recommend painting the full wall, corner to corner.

Do you paint after the repair?

Yes. Repair and paint by the same contractor is the main reason people call us. You get one quote and one visit schedule instead of booking two trades.

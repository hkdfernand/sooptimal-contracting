Process

How the job goes

You get a written scope before we start, and the same person on site every day.

  1. Send the unit listUnit numbers, bedroom counts and move-in dates. We reply with a per-unit price and a start date.
  2. Document the unitWe photograph every room before touching it, so damage beyond normal wear is on record for you.
  3. Repair and paintWalls repaired first, then ceilings, walls, trim and doors in your standard colours.
  4. Hand back with a reportYou get after photos and one invoice per unit that lists the base scope and any extras separately.

Questions

Rental unit turnovers FAQ

How is turnover work priced?

By unit size. A base price covers a standard repaint and normal wear repairs for a one, two or three bedroom unit. Damage beyond normal wear is listed and priced as a separate line before we do it.

How fast can you turn a unit?

A standard one or two bedroom unit is normally two to three working days from keys to hand-back. Heavy damage adds time and we tell you on day one.

Do you work as a subcontractor for larger contractors?

Yes. We currently do painting and drywall subcontract work on multi-unit residential buildings and can invoice per unit with photo documentation.

Which areas do you cover for multi-unit work?

Kitchener, Waterloo, Cambridge and Guelph, and Kingston for multi-unit projects.

## 📞 Phone Response Script

### **Opening** (Always answer professionally):
> "Good [morning/afternoon], SoOptimal Contracting, this is Fernand. How can I help you today?"

### **If they found you on HomeStars**:
> "Thank you for finding us on HomeStars! I see you're looking for drywall work. What type of project are you planning?"

### **Information to Gather**:
- [ ] Project type (new installation, repair, finishing)
- [ ] Property type (residential/commercial)
- [ ] Square footage or room count
- [ ] Timeline ("When do you need this completed?")
- [ ] Location (confirm it's in your service area)
- [ ] Budget range (optional)
- [ ] Their phone number for follow-up

### **Closing Every Call**:
> "I'd love to provide you with a free, no-obligation estimate. I can come by [suggest specific day/time] to take a look and give you an exact quote. Would [day] at [time] work for you?"

## 💼 Quote Preparation Checklist

### **Before the Site Visit**:
- [ ] Confirm appointment 1 day before
- [ ] Bring measuring tape, calculator, business cards
- [ ] Review current material prices
- [ ] Prepare estimate form/tablet

### **During the Site Visit**:
- [ ] Measure accurately (length x height for walls)
- [ ] Check for electrical/plumbing obstacles
- [ ] Assess prep work needed
- [ ] Take photos for records
- [ ] Discuss timeline and availability
- [ ] Present written estimate before leaving

### **Quote Follow-up**:
- [ ] Email written estimate within 24 hours
- [ ] Include start date options
- [ ] Follow up in 3-5 days if no response
- [ ] Add to CRM/tracking system

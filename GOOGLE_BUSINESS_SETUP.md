# 📊 Google Search Console Setup

## Step 1: Add Property
1. Go to: https://search.google.com/search-console/
2. Click "Add Property"
3. Choose "URL prefix" 
4. Enter: `https://sooptimal.com`

## Step 2: Verify Ownership
**HTML Tag Method (Recommended):**
1. Copy the meta tag provided
2. Add to website head section (I'll help with this)
3. Click "Verify"

**Alternative: DNS Verification**
- Add TXT record to domain DNS
- Copy the DNS record provided
- Add to domain settings

## Step 3: Submit Sitemap
1. Once verified, go to "Sitemaps" in left menu
2. Submit: `https://sooptimal.com/sitemap.xml`
3. Click "Submit"
